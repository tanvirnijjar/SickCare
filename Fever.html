🤒 Fever Care Guide

Feeling feverish? Here are some simple remedies, foods, drinks, and care tips that may help you feel better.

🍎 Foods to Eat

🍌 Banana
🍚 Khichdi
🍲 Vegetable Soup
🍎 Apple

🥤 Drinks

💧 Water
🥥 Coconut Water
🧂 ORS Solution
🍵 Herbal Tea

💚 Home Remedies

😴 Get plenty of rest
🧊 Use a cool wet cloth
🚿 Take a lukewarm bath
🌬️ Keep the room ventilated

🚫 Avoid

🍔 Junk Food
🥤 Cold Drinks
🍟 Oily Foods
⚠️ If fever is high, severe, or persistent, consult a healthcare professional.
