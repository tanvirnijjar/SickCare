🍽️ Indigestion Care Guide

Indigestion can cause stomach discomfort, bloating, and a feeling of fullness. Here are some foods, drinks, and habits that may help.

🥗 Foods to Eat

🍌 Banana
🍚 Plain Rice
🍎 Apple
🥣 Oatmeal
🥔 Boiled Potato

🥤 Drinks

💧 Water
🍵 Ginger Tea
🍋 Lemon Water
🌿 Herbal Tea

💚 Helpful Habits

🚶 Take a Short Walk
🍽️ Eat Slowly
😴 Get Proper Rest
🧘 Relax and Reduce Stress

🚫 Avoid

🌶️ Spicy Foods
🍔 Fast Food
🥤 Fizzy Drinks
🍟 Oily Foods
⚠️ Consult a healthcare professional if symptoms are severe, frequent, or persistent.
💡 Health Tip: Eating smaller meals and avoiding overeating may help reduce indigestion.
