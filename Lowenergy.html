🪫 Low Energy Care Guide

Feeling tired or low on energy? Proper nutrition, hydration, sleep, and exercise can help improve your energy levels.

🥗 Energy-Boosting Foods

🍌 Banana
🥚 Eggs
🥜 Nuts
🥛 Milk
🍎 Apple

🥤 Healthy Drinks

💧 Water
🥥 Coconut Water
🥛 Milk
🍊 Fresh Juice

💚 Healthy Habits

😴 Get 7–9 Hours Sleep
🏃 Regular Exercise
🚶 Take Short Walks
🧘 Manage Stress

🚫 Avoid

🍔 Too Much Junk Food
🥤 Excess Sugary Drinks
🌙 Staying Up Late
📱 Too Much Screen Time
⚠️ Consult a healthcare professional if tiredness is severe, persistent, or affects daily activities.
💡 Health Tip: Drinking enough water and getting quality sleep are two of the easiest ways to improve energy levels.
