🥴 Constipation Care Guide

Constipation can often be improved with proper hydration, fiber-rich foods, and healthy daily habits.

🥗 Foods to Eat

🍎 Apple
🍐 Pear
🥬 Leafy Greens
🥣 Oats
🍌 Banana

🥤 Drinks

💧 Plenty of Water
🥥 Coconut Water
🍋 Lemon Water
🥛 Warm Milk

💚 Healthy Habits

🚶 Daily Walking
🏃 Light Exercise
😴 Good Sleep
🧘 Stress Management

🚫 Avoid

🍔 Junk Food
🍟 Excess Fried Foods
🥤 Too Many Sugary Drinks
⚠️ Consult a healthcare professional if constipation is severe, persistent, or accompanied by concerning symptoms.
💡 Health Tip: Drinking enough water and eating fiber-rich foods may help maintain regular bowel movements.
