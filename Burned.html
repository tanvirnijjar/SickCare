🔥 Burned Skin Care Guide

Burned skin needs proper care to reduce pain and support healing. Follow these simple tips for minor burns.

✅ Helpful Remedies

💧 Cool Running Water
🧴 Aloe Vera Gel
🩹 Clean Bandage
🥤 Stay Hydrated

🥗 Healthy Foods

🍊 Vitamin C Fruits
🥚 Eggs
🥛 Milk
🥦 Vegetables

🚫 Avoid

🧊 Ice Directly on Burn
🧈 Butter or Oil
☀️ Direct Sunlight
🤚 Scratching the Area
⚠️ Seek medical help if the burn is severe, large, infected, or causes severe pain.
💡 Health Tip: Cool running water for 10–20 minutes can help reduce pain from a minor burn.
