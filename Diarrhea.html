💧 Diarrhea Care Guide

Diarrhea can cause dehydration and weakness. Drinking enough fluids and eating light foods can help recovery.

🥗 Foods to Eat

🍚 Plain Rice
🍌 Banana
🍞 Toast
🥔 Boiled Potatoes

🥤 Drinks

💧 Water
🧂 ORS Solution
🥥 Coconut Water
🍵 Clear Soup

🚫 Avoid

🌶️ Spicy Foods
🍔 Junk Food
🥤 Soft Drinks
☕ Too Much Coffee
⚠️ Seek medical attention if diarrhea lasts more than 2 days, contains blood, or causes severe dehydration.
💡 Health Tip: ORS (Oral Rehydration Solution) helps replace lost fluids and minerals.
