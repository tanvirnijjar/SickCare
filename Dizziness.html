😵 Dizziness Care Guide

Dizziness can make you feel lightheaded or unsteady. Rest, hydration, and proper nutrition may help.

🥗 Foods to Eat

🍌 Banana
🍎 Apple
🥚 Eggs
🥜 Nuts
🥗 Healthy Meals

🥤 Drinks

💧 Water
🥥 Coconut Water
🧂 ORS Solution
🍊 Fresh Juice

💚 Helpful Tips

😴 Get Enough Rest
🪑 Sit Down Slowly
🚶 Move Carefully
💧 Stay Hydrated

🚫 Avoid

🚭 Smoking
🍺 Alcohol
🏃 Sudden Movements
🥤 Too Much Caffeine
⚠️ Seek medical attention if dizziness is severe, frequent, causes fainting, or comes with chest pain.
💡 Health Tip: Dehydration is a common cause of dizziness. Drink enough water throughout the day.
